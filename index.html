About Guruji

Detailed Biography & Sacred Lineage

Guruji Maneesh Madhusudanan Potti represents the ancient temple priest lineage of Kerala. Born in Thrissur, the cultural capital of Kerala, he was trained from childhood in the traditional Gurukula style under his father and grandfather, mastering Rigveda and Atharvaveda recitation and Kerala's Tanthrasamuchaya ritual rules.

For over 20 years, Guruji has performed complex Vedic rituals (Homas, Sevas, and Consecrations) and analyzed birth charts (Jyotisham) to guide seekers towards peace and prosperity. His family lineage belongs to the respected temple priests (Thanthris) of central Kerala.

Since 2012, Guruji has visited Russia regularly, establishing a large community of spiritual seekers in Moscow and Saint Petersburg. He conducts physical pujas and online consultations, bridging Eastern Vedic wisdom with Western seekers.

Credentials & Accomplishments

  • ✦ Rigveda & Yajurveda Adhyayana: Certified recitation and ritual mastery under Brahmaswom Madhom, Thrissur.
  • ✦ Tantric Consecrations: Conducted and assisted in over 15 temple Pratishtas (consecrations) across Kerala.
  • ✦ Astrology Consultations: 10,000+ personal horoscopes matched and analyzed globally.
  • ✦ Cultural Bridges: Recognized by spiritual societies in India and Russia for his work in spreading Vedic knowledge.
